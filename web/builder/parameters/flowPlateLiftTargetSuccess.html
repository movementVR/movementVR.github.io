Target size [m]
  (max distance plate-target center)
Max plate tilt [deg]
(Pitch=X=front-back; Yaw=Y=flat rotation; Roll=Z=left-right)
Hold time [s]
Phase 4 = Plate Lift
 1) Participants are actively lifting plate to target
 2) "Target reached" when:
   a) Distance between plate & target centers becomes less than "Target size"
 3) "Success" when:
   a) Distance between plate & target centers remains less than "Target size"
   b) Plate tilt remains less than "Max plate tilt"
   c) Both conditions a) and b) remain true for "Hold time"
 4) "Failure" when:
   a) Conditions 2a (distance) or 2b (tilt) become false after "Target reached"
   b) Plate falls / exists workspace (edit under "workspace")
   c) Phase or trial max time limits are reached (edit under "transitions")
 5) Phase ends when Success (3) or Failure (4) occurs
