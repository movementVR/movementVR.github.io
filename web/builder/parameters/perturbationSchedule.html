How to define the  Gain Pertubation Schedule:
Option 1: Use this tab (Paradigm Tab) to define a standard adaptation paradigm

 • zero in baseline (no perturbation)
 • gradually ramp up in gradual adaptation
 • max - equal to total perturbation - at adaptation plateau
 • zero in washout (no perturbation)
Option 2: Click the gear icon on the next tab to customize gain trial-by-trial
