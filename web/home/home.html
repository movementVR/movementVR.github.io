MovementVR is a platform to create motor control and learning experiments in immersive Virtual Reality without any coding.

Why MovementVR?

We have all heard the goal: “Make experiments more like the real world”. So why is there still a gap between lab studies and how people move in daily life?

After weeks of wrestling with libraries, rebuilding models, and rewriting code just to get virtual hands working, we hit an inevitable truth: building natural, immersive motor tasks is hard, slow, and resource-costly.

That is why we created MovementVR: open source, coding-free, and designed to make realistic motor tasks easy to build and customize. Do not worry — you can still dive into the code and Unity project to tweak it or completely reinvent it — however you like.

What is MovementVR?

MovementVR is a standalone solution for motor learning experiments in VR.. It includes a ready-to-use VR app with a naturalistic motor task, plus tools to customize the task and experiment and process the recorded data.

Feed the bird in the VR app: lift a plate of grapes up to a target with a bird perched on it. You use your own hands — tracked in real time and fully articulated in VR — to grasp and move the plate just as you would in real life.

Build your own Feed the Bird experiment using the code-free Builder GUI: define task parameters, adaptation and feedback rules, trial sequences and experimental phases, and more. After the experiment, use our Analyzer tools to process the automatically-recorded hand kinematic data.

Getting Started
  1. Design your experiment using MovementVR Builder
  2. Deploy and run your customized MovementVR Application in your VR headset
  3. Analyze your results with the help of our MovementVR Analyzer
