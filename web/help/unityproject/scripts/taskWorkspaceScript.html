Documentation: taskWorkspaceScript.cs

1. Overview

The taskWorkspaceScript monitors whether the plate leaves the designated task workspace area without entering the target. It is responsible for triggering a specific failure state (-5) when the participant reaches outside bounds but fails to make contact with the target. This supports spatial constraint enforcement during a trial.

2. Functional Blocks

Initialization

  • startup() retrieves references to the plate trigger, targetScript, and trialScript from the scene hierarchy.

Trigger Monitoring

  • OnTriggerExit() detects if the plate leaves the workspace without having successfully entered the target.
  • If triggered, it invokes a stage transition (-5) and sets the target mesh to unsuccessful state.
