Documentation: birdScript.cs

1. Overview

The birdScript class simulates a realistic animated bird in Unity, including behavior transitions such as takeoff, flight, landing, and idle ground behavior. It uses animations, sound effects, randomized motion, and collision avoidance to emulate natural bird behavior. The bird moves between predefined targets in the scene and adapts based on spatial and timing conditions.

2. Functional Blocks

Initialization

On start, the bird is disabled and scheduled to initialize after a random delay. It sets up audio volumes, picks a spawn location, and triggers the first flight.

Update Loop

A state machine checks the bird's current stage (ground, takeoff, flight, landing) and triggers the corresponding behavior and transition logic.

Stage Transitions

  • toStage0: Transition to idle ground state after landing.
  • toStage1: Initiates takeoff after spending time on the target.
  • toStage2: Begins flight after takeoff duration.
  • toStage3: Initiates landing near target.

Motion Behaviors

  • motionGround: Randomly triggers idle animations like singing, pecking, hopping, etc.
  • motionTakeoff: Applies upward and forward force to simulate takeoff.
  • motionFlying: Rotates bird, applies thrust, and avoids obstacles.
  • motionLanding: Smooths descent and rotation for landing.

Helpers

  • changeTarget(): Selects new random target and position.
  • ResetHopInt(): Resets hopping animation flag.
  • PlaySong(): Randomly plays one of the bird's songs.
