MovementVR: Installation & Export Guide

This guide walks you through installing the MovementVR app, uploading your configuration, running the app, and exporting recorded data. This assumes you have already created your experimental configuration and have your parameters.csv file ready. If not, please return to the Builder tab.

If you’ve already set up Hand Tracking and Developer Mode, you can jump to Step 1. Install MovementVR App via SideQuest. Otherwise, expand the Headset Setup Prerequisites sections below.

Headset Setup Prerequisites: Hand Tracking

To continue with the MovementVR app setup, your headset must have Hand Tracking enabled—this lets you navigate with your hands instead of controllers. You can follow the official Meta instructions or use the steps below:

  1. Wear your headset and open Quick Settings by clicking the clock in the bottom-left corner of the universal menu (if the menu isn’t visible, press the Meta/Oculus button on your right controller).
  2. Open Settings by clicking the gear icon in the top-right corner of Quick Settings.
  3. In Settings, click Movement tracking (subtitled “Hand Tracking”).
  4. Toggle Hand and body tracking ON, then follow the on-screen prompts to finish setup.
Headset Setup Prerequisites: Developer Mode

To continue with the MovementVR app setup, your headset must have Developer Mode enabled. If it is not enabled — or you are unsure whether it is — there are three easy ways to set it up:

Option 1 (recommended, especially if you're unsure of Developer Mode status)

Simply continue with the rest of the MovementVR setup. When you install SideQuest, the platform will automatically detect whether Developer Mode is enabled. If it is not, SideQuest provides clear step-by-step instructions and videos to guide you through the process.

Option 2

Follow the official instructions for setting up your device and enabling Developer Mode, available here:  https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/native/android/mobile-device-setup

Option 3

The steps below were written in May 2025 and may change over time. Search online for “Enable Developer Mode on Meta Quest 2” (or your model) for the most up-to-date instructions.

  1. Make sure the Meta Horizon app is installed on your phone or tablet, and that you remember the login credentials for the Meta account linked to your headset (this is the same app and account used during initial headset setup — it was previously called the Meta Quest or Oculus app).
  2. Set up a Meta Developer account by following the steps at  https://developers.meta.com/horizon/manage/organizations/create/ . Log in with the same Meta account used for your headset and Meta app. You may be asked to complete two-factor authentication (common authenticator apps, text message, or credit-card verification all work). Finally, create your “Developer Organization” by choosing a name.
  3. Enable Developer Mode in the Meta Horizon app:
    Tap  Menu → Devices → (select your headset) → Headset settings → Developer Mode → Toggle ON.

  4. Connect your headset to your computer and put it on. You should be prompted to tap "Allow USB debugging" — check Always allow from this computer to avoid future prompts. If you don’t see the prompt, unplug → re-plug or restart the headset. See the  Meta documentation for any additional settings or drivers you may need.
Step 1. Install MovementVR App via SideQuest
  1. Download the MovementVR APK file:
  2. Download & Install the SideQuest Desktop App on your computer:  https://sidequestvr.com/setup-howto
  3. Open SideQuest
  4. Launch the SideQuest APK Installer by clicking the icon  labeled "Install APK file from folder on computer" in the top navigation bar.
  5. When prompted, select the downloaded MovementVR.apk file.
Step 2. Launch the App Once to Create Folders
  1. Open the App Manager by clicking the icon  labeled "Currently installed apps" in the top navigation bar.
  2. Locate com.MovementVR.MovementVR and click the gear icon 
  3. In the popup, click "Launch App" 
  4. Put on the headset & wait for the app to start.
  5. Once it has launched, quit the app as you would any other app on the headset.
  6. Return to SideQuest and click "Done"  to close the popup.

This ensures the internal app folder is created.

Step 3. Upload Your parameters.csv File to the App
  1. Open the File Manager by clicking the icon  labeled "Manage files on the headset" in the top navigation bar.
  2. Navigate to: Android/data/com.MovementVR.MovementVR/files
  3. Click the  button labeled "Add file" at the bottom-right corner. In the floating menu, click  ("Upload file to this folder").
  4. Select your parameters.csv file containing your experiment configuration.
Step 4. Running the App
  1. Disconnect your headset from the computer and put it on.
  2. Inside the headset, open the App Library by clicking the icon  in the universal menu. (If the universal menu is hidden, click the Meta  or Oculus  icons to open it.)
  3. Locate the dropdown at the top-right of the App Library (labelled “All” or another filter). If it’s not visible, click the Search bar at the top to reveal it.
  4. In the dropdown, select "Unknown Sources".
    If “Unknown Sources” is missing, ensure Developer Mode is enabled and search online for updated instructions as these settings often change.
  5. Find the MovementVR app and click it to run.
    Tip: switch from controllers to hand-tracking before starting the app; switching inside the app can sometimes be glitchy.
Step 5. Export the Recorded Data

Congratulations on running your first MovementVR experiment! The steps below explain how to export the data recorded by the app.

  1. Reconnect the headset to the computer and open SideQuest.
  2. In SideQuest, open the File Manager  ( in the top navigation bar) and navigate to  Android/data/com.MovementVR.MovementVR/files.
  3. You should see your data folder(s) with names formatted as  data_YYYY_MM_DD_HH_mm_ss. Click the save icon  to the right of a folder to export it.
  4. Click Select location  to choose where to save your data, then click Save to PC 

Next: proceed to the Analyzer tab on the MovementVR website to learn how to process your data.
